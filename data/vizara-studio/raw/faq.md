## Do you offer refunds if I'm not happy with the result?
We don't offer refunds, but we do offer revisions — depending on your plan, you can request changes until the visual matches your vision (2 revision phases on Starter, unlimited on Growth).

## Can I get my order rushed faster than the standard turnaround?
Yes. If you need your visuals sooner than your plan's stated delivery window, rush delivery is available for an additional fee — reach out before starting and we'll quote a faster turnaround for your deadline.

## Do you work with international clients?
Yes — Vizara Studio works with clients anywhere in the world, not just locally.

## Can you sign an NDA if my product needs to stay confidential before launch?
Yes. If your product hasn't launched yet and needs to stay private, we're happy to sign an NDA before any files or product details are shared.

## What do you need from me to get started — photos, or the physical product?
It depends on the project. For some products, photos or product details are enough for our visual engines to work with. For others, we may ask you to ship the physical product so we can capture it directly — we'll let you know which applies once we see what you're working with.

## Do you send raw/unedited files, or only the final version?
Only the final version. Most projects start from a plain product photo and are built into a full cinematic visual from scratch, so there's no "raw" file to hand over — the finished cinematic piece is the deliverable.

## What file formats do I receive?
PNG or JPG for image deliverables, MP4 for video ads. SVG can be provided on request if needed.

## Do you edit personal or non-product photos?
We work exclusively with product images — portraits, personal photos, and non-product content aren't something we offer.
