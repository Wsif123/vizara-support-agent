# How it works

## Video ads
1. Send your product photos (or footage, if you have it)
2. Tell Wasif the style or energy you want — or let him suggest one
3. Receive your finished 20-second cinematic video ad

## Product photography
1. Message first so Wasif can confirm your product is a good fit, then order and send your product photo
2. Tell Wasif your preferred style or mood
3. Receive your cinematic product visual

## Before ordering, either service
Any product photo works, regardless of its original angle, lighting, or quality. If you're unsure what style fits your brand, message Wasif first — he'll suggest a direction before any work starts.
