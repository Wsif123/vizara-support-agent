# Pricing

## Video ads — "Cinematic 20-second video ad"

**Basic — $30**
- 4-day delivery, 1 revision
- Video editing, script writing
- 20 seconds running time, 1 length variation, 1 size/orientation
- Product imagery, voice-over recording

**Standard — $60**
- 3-day delivery, 2 revisions
- Video editing, script writing
- 20 seconds running time, 2 length variations, 2 size/orientations
- Product imagery, voice-over recording

**Premium — $80**
- 2-day delivery, 3 revisions
- Video editing, script writing
- 20 seconds running time, 3 length variations, 3 size/orientations
- Product imagery, voice-over recording

General terms across all tiers: 1 revision on Basic, 2 on Standard, 3 on Premium — additional revisions available as a paid add-on. Rush delivery is available as an add-on if you need it faster than your tier's stated turnaround.
