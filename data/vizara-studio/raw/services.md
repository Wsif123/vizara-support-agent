# Services

Vizara Studio offers two core services, delivered by Wasif Nizam (Founder), sold via Fiverr.

## 1. Cinematic product video ads
A 20-second cinematic video ad built from your product photos — raw footage works too if you have it, but isn't required. Includes multi-angle shots and transitions, your choice of music (or Wasif selects something that fits the vibe), and optional AI-generated voice-over narration with a choice of natural-sounding voice styles.

Delivered in whichever orientation matches your platform: vertical (9:16) for Instagram/TikTok Reels, square (1:1) for feed posts, or horizontal (16:9) for YouTube ads.

**Best for:** D2C brands launching a new product, Amazon/Daraz sellers wanting a scroll-stopping ad, beauty/fragrance/food & beverage/fashion brands, or anyone whose static product photos aren't converting.

## 2. Cinematic product photography
A single cinematic product visual built from your actual product photo — not a generic mockup. Includes lifestyle staging, photoshoot styling, lighting, photo retouching, and background removal, tailored to your brand. High-resolution output ready for Instagram, Amazon, Daraz, or print.

**Best for:** Daraz & Amazon sellers, food & beverage brands, beauty & fragrance products, skincare & personal care, snacks & packaged goods, and any product that needs to look more premium than its raw photo.

## Not sure what style fits?
Message before ordering with a description of your product and brand vibe, and Wasif will suggest a direction — clean/luxury, energetic/hype, playful, or something else — before work starts. Any product photo works regardless of its original angle, lighting, or quality.
