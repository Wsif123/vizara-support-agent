# AI Chatbot Service (Vizara Studio)

## What it is
Vizara Studio also builds custom AI chatbots for business websites. Each chatbot is trained specifically on the client's own business content (FAQs, pricing, policies) and answers customer questions instantly, 24/7. It escalates to a human when it doesn't know the answer instead of guessing.

## Pricing

### Standard — $49/month (+ $75 one-time setup)
- Up to ~15 pages/FAQ worth of content
- Default widget styling, brand colors only
- Standard tone/persona
- 1 round of content revisions after launch
- Email escalation handoff
- Delivery: 2–3 business days from form submission to live bot

### Premium — $79/month (+ $130 one-time setup)
- Up to ~40 pages/FAQ worth of content
- Fully brand-matched widget (colors, fonts, positioning)
- Custom persona/tone tuning
- Priority handling on content updates
- Monthly usage summary (conversation volume, common questions)
- Delivery: 3–5 business days (more content to ingest, custom widget styling, tone tuning)

## How it works
1. Client fills out a short onboarding form with their business info, FAQs, pricing, and preferred tone.
2. Vizara Studio sets up the chatbot, trains it on the client's content, and matches it to their brand.
3. Client receives a widget snippet to add to their site (or Vizara installs it for them).
4. Bot goes live, answering real customer questions grounded in the client's actual content.

## What the bot can and can't do
It can answer policy, pricing, FAQ, and product-info questions accurately based on what it's trained on. It does not have access to live order tracking or real-time inventory — those still require a human reply for now.

## Who it's for
Any business with a website that gets repetitive customer questions — ecommerce stores (shipping, returns, sizing), service businesses (pricing, availability), and general small businesses wanting 24/7 instant responses without hiring extra support staff.
